layers

Three layers, each with one job. Glass reads as glass only when content moves under it, so it stays on the layer that floats.

ground

The lattice and the page. Slow jaali shapes can drift behind a hub so glass has colour to bend.

content

Flat tiles, big type, hairlines. No blur, so text stays crisp on any screen.

function

Glass on bars, pills, menus, sheets and toasts. It floats over content and never stacks on glass.

  • glass goes onBars, tab pills, menus, sheets, toasts, and glass tiles over a stage.
  • flat stays onTiles you read, lists, text panels and page backgrounds.
  • one layerGlass never sits on glass. A menu opened from a bar takes over the bar's job.
  • ink on glassText on glass stays ink. The pop marks state with a dot, an edge or a fill, never body text.
  • materializeGlass arrives by ramping its blur from 0 to 24px while it rises, then leaves the same way.

three colours

Base holds the ground and plain tiles. The primary fills live tiles and the lattice. The pop marks only what needs you: a held dot, a flag, one tile per hub. Four themes are built this way; the other eight reuse their accent as the pop.

liquid

Liquid motion answers an action. Edges lead and trail, shapes overshoot and settle, glass rises out of blur. Nothing here moves on its own except the melting word.

stretch.

stretch(box). The leading edge moves in 0.36s and the trailing edge in 0.62s.

grow.

grow(button). The + grows into its own row of actions with a spring.

melt.

jaali in

melt(el). One word melts into the next through a threshold filter. Holds still under reduced motion.

materialize.

materialize(el). Blur ramps from 0 to 24px while the toast rises.

worm.

worm(scroller, pager). The dot stretches to the next one as you scroll sideways, then its tail follows.

hub

Live tiles over a stage. Glass tiles blur the shapes moving behind them. Tap one and it pours out into a sheet; close it and it pours back. Example data.

use it

Glass and liquid ship inside jaali.css and jaali.js. No extra files.

<!-- a hub with glass tiles over a stage -->
<div class="j-staged">
  <div class="j-stage"></div>
  <div class="j-hub">
    <div class="j-live is-glass">...</div>   <!-- or is-pop, is-accent -->
  </div>
</div>

import { stage, sheen, stretch, grow, pour, worm, melt, materialize } from './jaali/jaali.js';
stage(document.querySelector('.j-stage'));
sheen();
pour(tile, (body) => { body.innerHTML = '<h4>details</h4>'; });
