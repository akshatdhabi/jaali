left outTried and removed.

  • 45-degree chamfersLooked like a different brand.
  • Cloud-shaped archesPointed Amer arches only.
  • Condensed caps and quirky display facesGeist, thin, lowercase.
  • Mono all-caps labelsSentence case captions.
  • Pastel status coloursStatus is told by form.
  • Dark mode as inverted lightDark is designed on its own.
  • Titles that slide off screenTitles stay in frame.
  • Pop-ins in the same colour as the surface belowRaised has its own colour.
  • A liquid drop that follows the cursorIt pulled the eye off the content. Liquid motion only answers an action.
  • Glass panes over reading contentText under blur is hard to read. Glass stays on what floats.
  • Water dropletsThey clashed with the lattice. Liquid moves through edges and shapes, never drops.
